Priced by final
edited length

Reaction Video pricing is based on the final edited video length, not the original VOD or source footage length.

For example, a 2-hour VOD edited into a 35-minute Reaction Video falls under the 21–40 minute tier ($60). Exceptionally long source footage, extensive footage review, or editing beyond the standard Reaction scope may need a custom quote.

If the project needs full subtitles, frequent visual effects, memes, motion graphics, or heavy editing throughout, it will be quoted as Long-form editing instead.
