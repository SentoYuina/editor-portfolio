Estimated turnaround

Good edits take a little time.

  • Short-form videos: approximately 3–7 business days.
  • Long-form videos: approximately 2–4 weeks.
  • Reaction Videos: approximately 1–2 weeks per video.
  • Turnaround times are estimates and may vary depending on workload, project complexity, and queue.

  • If additional time is needed, I’ll let you know as soon as possible.
Footage & content selection

What to send

Short-form

VOD scrubbing is not included. Please provide timestamps, a timeline, or Twitch Clips for the moments you want edited.

Long-form

VOD scrubbing and content selection are included. Send the full VOD and I’ll review it and select suitable content.

Reaction Videos

Full source footage may be provided. Pricing assumes a reasonable amount of source footage relative to the expected final video. Exceptionally long VODs or extensive footage searching may require a Custom Quote.

Editing

What’s included

Short-form

Full subtitles, visual effects, editing, and pacing throughout the entire video.

Long-form

Full subtitles, visual effects, editing, and pacing throughout the entire video.

Reaction Videos

Enhanced editing, subtitles, and visual effects focus on the first 1–2 minutes. The remainder receives basic cutting and pacing; full subtitles and effects throughout are not included.

Additional assets such as memes, reaction images, character assets, or other materials are welcome and can help improve the final edit.

Revisions

Reasonable revisions are included.

Small adjustments such as fixing mistakes, changing individual cuts, adjusting subtitles, or making minor visual changes are included.

Major changes to the original brief, significant restructuring, or requests that substantially increase the editing scope may require an additional fee. Please communicate major changes as early as possible.

Payment

PayPal only

Payment is accepted via PayPal only and is due after the video is completed.

The final completed video will be delivered after payment has been received.

For larger projects, bundles, or ongoing commissions, payment arrangements may be discussed separately.
